# Placement Examples

## Medical hero

A medical motion asset is placed at the logical trailing edge in RTL, large on desktop, centered on tablet, and replaced by its static preview for mobile/reduced-motion. Trust icons remain supporting content and cannot compete with the heading.

## Manufacturing features

Factory/production objects belong in showcases or About. Compact engineering and quality-control icons belong in feature cards. A large looping factory motion inside each feature card is rejected for hierarchy and performance.

## Restaurant products

Food objects and menu visuals use contained card media. Lifestyle visuals may support Hero or About. Decorative food objects must not become unlabeled functional controls.

## Personal brand contact

Communication/social icons are inline, RTL-aware, small, and accessible. A professional motion can support Hero but is not accepted in Footer because motion is excluded there.

## Ecommerce products

Product objects use contained card media; shopping motion is reserved for Hero/CTA; payment and delivery icons support Features. Theme adaptation remains a proposal until contrast validation and approval.

Executable valid and invalid fixtures live in `tools/smart-template-enhancement-engine/test-scenarios/placement/`.
