# AI Creative Assistant Contract

OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, local models, and other providers connect only through future adapters. AI may suggest scenes, storytelling, assets, animation style, and quality improvements. Output is untrusted proposal data.

AI cannot modify Master Assets, change ownership, approve, publish, deploy, bypass quality/security rules, or access unrelated/private sources. Inputs are minimized and provider-neutral; credentials remain server-held in any future implementation.
