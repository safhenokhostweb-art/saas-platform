# Creation Wizard Flow

1. Purpose: brand, product, education, service, advertisement, social, website hero, or UI explanation.
2. Audience: general, professional, customers, students, or internal training.
3. Style: modern, minimal, corporate, luxury, Iranian traditional, technology, or friendly.
4. Duration: 5, 10, 15, 30, or 60+ seconds.
5. Language/context, brand tokens, educational and optional voice choices.

Beginner mode recommends defaults and explains tradeoffs. Expert mode exposes timeline, layers, keyframes, curves, and scene controls, but cannot bypass safety or quality blockers.
