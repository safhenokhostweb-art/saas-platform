# Motion Quality Score Model

Weighted dimensions total 100: storytelling 20%, typography 18%, animation 18%, brand compatibility 16%, performance 14%, accessibility 14%. The final score is the rounded weighted result.

90–100: premium review candidate; 80–89: good with warnings; 70–79: revision required; below 70 or any blocker: blocked. Automated scores are evidence, not publication approval; human creative review remains required.
