# Reuse Policy

Platform Masters are reusable within license and compatibility rules. Template packages may be reused by compatible sites but each installation receives a distinct site manifest and authorization boundary.

Customer assets are private by default. Customer A content, variants, logos, prompts, package paths, or metadata must never be directly referenced by Customer B.

Platform Admin may promote a customer-derived design only after verifying customer rights/consent, removing logos and private data, security and originality review, license assignment, structural normalization, and creation of a **new** template or Platform Master lineage. Promotion never relabels or exposes the private original.

Safe reuse options are compiled-byte deduplication behind separate ownership records, cloning from an approved generic reusable template into a new site lineage, or publishing a new reviewed Platform Master. Similar brand palettes do not authorize reuse.
